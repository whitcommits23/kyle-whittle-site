My Story

Throughout my career, leadership and developing people have been at the center of everything I do. Over the past decade I've built onboarding programs, designed training systems, and led teams across companies like Qualtrics, McMaster-Carr, Canopy, and Entrata — and a key part of every role was coaching people up. Helping someone see what's holding them back, giving them a framework to move forward, and holding them accountable to the work.

That's what eventually made coaching a natural next step. The same skills I used to develop teams at scale — systems thinking, structured feedback, clear accountability — are exactly what individuals need to get unstuck. Pair that with Stoic philosophy and a background in Instructional Design and I/O Psychology, and you get a coaching approach that's more framework than fluff.
