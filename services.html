What I Offer

  • 1-on-1 Life Coaching Regular sessions focused on clarity, accountability, and forward motion. We identify what's blocking you and build a plan to move through it.
  • Stoicism-Based Coaching Programs A structured program applying Stoic philosophy to modern life challenges — designed for people who want a durable framework, not a quick fix.
  • Workshop & Group Sessions Group settings for teams or cohorts looking to build a shared vocabulary around resilience, decision-making, and personal responsibility.

How to Work With Me

  1. Reach out Send me an email with a few sentences about where you are and what you're trying to change.
  2. Discovery call (free) We talk for 30 minutes. You ask questions, I ask questions, and we figure out if working together makes sense.
  3. Coaching begins If it's a fit, we establish a cadence and get to work. No long commitments up front — just show up and do the work.
